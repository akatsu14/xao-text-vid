A TIARA FOR A NEW TIME

Her wedding day was the first chapter, and the engagement ring was the prologue. When Catherine stepped out of Westminster Abbey in 2011, the world held its breath. It was a sign of love and duty during a period of constitutional instability. It had 739 brilliant-cut and 149 baguette diamonds and was beautiful but not too flashy. It was a "starter" tiara that showed humility and respect for the institution she was joining. It was a choice that showed respect, as if the bride knew her place in the big, developing pageant of monarchy.

Twelve years later, King Charles III was crowned. Things had changed in the globe, and so had her job.
