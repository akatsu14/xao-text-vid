Camilla represents the monarchy's past—a past of duty, discretion, and scandal overcome. Catherine represents its future—a future predicated on relatability, emotional intelligence, and media savvy. These two models of royal womanhood are not always compatible, and in the pressurized confines of palace life, it is almost inevitable that they would one day collide.
