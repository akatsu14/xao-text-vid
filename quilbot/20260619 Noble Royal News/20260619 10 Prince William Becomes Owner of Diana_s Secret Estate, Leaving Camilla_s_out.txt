The Duchess and The Crown A Legacy Of Unrivalled Value

The current definition of the Duchy of Cornwall is a concept that is difficult to pin down. It was established in 1337 by King Edward III for his son and heir, Edward, the Black Prince. The purpose of the institution was to provide the heir apparent with an independent income, so liberating him from dependence on the king or the state. It is a patchwork quilt of modern Britain that is kept together by medieval statute, and it encompasses a vast portfolio of land, property, and assets that spans across 128,000 acres in 23 counties. The range of its assets is astounding, since it includes ancient woodlands that have been around for centuries, rough coasts that have been battered by the Atlantic, wealthy commercial properties in downtown London, and even The Oval cricket venue, which is considered to be the cathedral of English sport. The kingdom of Althopp, which cannot be conquered
