THE LONELY CROWN PRICE

Instead of public announcement, institutional authority spoke quietly and authoritatively. Correction occurred the following week. The affair's final lesson was this. It wasn't about treachery or ambition—too simple. It showed the Crown's horrible, alienating logic. It taught the price of legitimacy, paid in public duty and individual sacrifice, and asked for time and again by those who carry a millennium of history on human shoulders.
