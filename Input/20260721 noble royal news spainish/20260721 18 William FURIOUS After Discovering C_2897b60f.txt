William unfolded the specialist's report and read the conclusion aloud. The language of medicine was cold and clinical, but the message was unequivocal. THE GUARDIAN OF THE THRONE

And as a delicate quiet was settling on everything, a message came from the doctors. Another brief episode of heart rhythm disturbance had occurred during the night for Charles. He would have been proud to see his son succeed, but it would have been the ultimate proof that the Crown did not need him any more. Hours later the palace announced a televised address by the King. At Windsor Charles sat by a window and made no attempt to conceal the extent of his weakness or to promise an impossible recovery. He thanked his medical team, the public and the family who had been forced to carry a burden he refused to let go of. Then, looking straight at the camera, he uttered the words that would shape the future of his reign. “The Crown works best when duty is shared, truth is spoken and love is allowed to do its work.” He indicated his public position would be scaled back forever. William, on the other hand, was not a replacement, but the active guardian of the reign, taking on its responsibilities, while maintaining his father’s dignity. When the record was finished William entered the room. The King asked, “Do you think that because I am honest I am weak?” "No," said his son. "It made you sound like my father." The monarchy had survived not because its King had seemed indestructible, but because, at the most crucial time of his life, his family had finally, forcefully let him be human.
